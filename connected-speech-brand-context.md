## 3. Services

### Children

**Language Delays**  
When language milestones are not being met, early intervention makes the biggest difference. We identify what is holding a child back and build the specific skills they need, so they can communicate their needs, connect with others, and thrive at school.

**Language & Literacy Development**  
Strong language skills are the foundation for reading and writing. We build the vocabulary, sentence structure, and phonological awareness a child needs to develop literacy skills and succeed at school.

**Articulation & Intelligibility**  
Speech that is difficult to understand can affect confidence and connection. We work on accurate sound production and clear speech patterns, so a person can be understood the first time, by anyone.

**Feeding Therapy**  
Mealtimes should not be a source of stress. We work with children who have difficulty with textures, swallowing, or oral motor skills, helping them eat more comfortably and confidently, so that mealtimes become easier for the whole family.

**Complex Communication Needs (AAC)**  
Some people communicate in ways that go beyond speech, and that is okay. We support children and adults with AAC tools, Makaton, PROMPT, and individualised strategies, so they can express themselves and participate fully in the world around them.

### Adults

**Stuttering & Stammering**  
Stuttering and stammering affect both children and adults, and the impact goes far beyond speech. Your therapist works with you and your family to build fluency, confidence, and the tools to communicate without fear, at any age.

**Stroke & Medical Conditions**  
We support adults recovering from strokes and other medical conditions that affect communication, helping them rebuild the skills they need to express themselves and reconnect with the people in their lives.

## 8. Brand Voice & Tone

### In three words
**Warm. Direct. Capable.**

### What it sounds like
Connected Speech speaks like a trusted professional who also happens to be a real person. The voice is confident without being clinical, warm without being saccharine, and inclusive without being performative.

- **Direct:** Short sentences. No filler. Says what it means.
- **Human:** Uses "you" and "we" — never "the practice" or "our clinicians"
- **Specific:** Real imagery over abstract language ("the day your child says mum for the first time", "understood at the dinner table")
- **Inclusive:** Acknowledges that communication looks different for everyone — words, signs, devices, whatever works
- **Hopeful, not pitying:** Parents and clients are partners, not people to be felt sorry for
- **Family-aware:** Recognises that a child's communication challenge affects the whole family

### What it does NOT sound like
- Clinical or jargon-heavy ("therapy outcomes", "evidence-based interventions")
- Corporate or cold
- Overly cheerful or motivational-poster-esque
- Preachy about inclusion — shows it, doesn't lecture

### Tone shifts by context
| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Website headlines | Poetic, confident, measured |
| Service descriptions | Clear, specific, benefit-led |
| FAQs | Warm, conversational, reassuring |
| Social media | Human, relatable, occasionally personal |
| Professional/clinical | Precise, credentialed, serious |

## 12. Content Pillars

Use these five pillars to guide content creation across all channels:

### 1. Education
Demystify speech-language pathology for parents and adults. What does a speech therapist actually do? What are the milestones? What is AAC? What does a session look like?

*Examples: "What age should my child start talking?", "What is Makaton and is it right for my child?", "What happens in a first speech therapy session?"*

### 2. Reassurance
Address the anxiety parents and adults feel when they first suspect there is a problem. Normalise seeking help. Celebrate early intervention.

*Examples: "You're not overreacting", "There's no such thing as seeking help too soon", "What to do if you're worried about your child's speech"*

### 3. Expertise
Establish Jodi as a credible, specialist voice in AAC, complex communication, and speech-language pathology in South Africa. Share knowledge. Reference her academic background and clinical experience.

*Examples: Makaton explainers, AAC device overviews, research-backed articles on early intervention*

### 4. Community & Inclusion
Celebrate communication in all its forms. Speak to families of children with autism, complex needs, AAC users. Show that the practice understands their world.

*Examples: "What it means to communicate without words", "How Makaton changed our family", stories from the school experience*

### 5. Practice Life
Human, behind-the-scenes content. The locations, the approach, the free screenings, why the practice was built the way it was.

*Examples: "Why we do home visits", "How free screenings work", "What Monday to Thursday looks like at Connected Speech"*

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Connected Speech?**  
The name refers to "connected speech" as a term in speech-language pathology — natural, continuous language as it occurs in real conversation. Assessing and improving connected speech is central to the practice because real communication happens in connection with other people.

**Do you have an office?**  
Sessions are available at private premises in Morningside, Sandton, as well as through home visits, school visits, and online.

**Do you accept medical aid?**  
Yes. Medical aid and cash accepted. Medical aid is submitted on the client's behalf — there is nothing to chase.

**What ages do you work with?**  
Children from birth through school age, and adults at any stage of life.

**What is AAC?**  
Augmentative and Alternative Communication — tools and strategies that support communication beyond speech. This includes Makaton signing, picture-based systems, and high-tech communication devices.

**How do I know if my child needs speech therapy?**  
If you have any concerns about your child's speech, language, feeding, or communication at any age, a consultation is always the right first step. There is no such thing as seeking help too soon.

**Do you offer free screenings?**  
Yes. Free screenings for individuals and for schools, with no commitment required.

## 15. Values (for content alignment)

All content should reflect these four values:

**Person-centred** — Every therapy plan is built around the individual: their needs, their pace, their world. Content should speak to the individual, not a category.

**Inclusive by design** — Communication takes many forms. Content should reflect and celebrate that — words, signs, devices, gestures.

**Family-first** — Families are partners in therapy, not bystanders. Content should speak to the people around the client as much as to the client themselves.

**Results you can see** — Real progress, reported clearly. Content should be specific and outcome-focused, not vague or aspirational.
