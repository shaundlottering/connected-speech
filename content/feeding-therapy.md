Feeding Therapy
Mealtimes should not be a source of stress.

Feeding therapy supports children who struggle with the physical or sensory side of eating, whether that shows up as difficulty with certain textures, trouble chewing or swallowing safely, or a mealtime that ends in tears more often than not. It is delivered by a speech-language pathologist because feeding and swallowing rely on many of the same oral muscles and coordination skills used in speech.

What feeding therapy addresses

Oral motor skills Some children have not yet developed the strength, coordination, or control in their jaw, tongue, and lips needed to chew and swallow a full range of foods safely and comfortably.

Texture difficulties A child may manage purées or soft foods well but struggle, gag, or refuse when textures change, which can narrow their diet and make family mealtimes stressful.

Swallowing difficulties Some children have genuine difficulty coordinating a safe swallow, which needs careful, professional support rather than pressure or persuasion at the table.

Selective or restricted eating When a limited diet is rooted in sensory or motor difficulty rather than simple fussiness, therapy addresses the underlying cause instead of just the behaviour at mealtimes.

We help children eat more comfortably and confidently, so that mealtimes become easier for the whole family.

How it works

1. A free screening We start with a free screening, with no cost and no commitment required, to get a clear picture of what is happening at mealtimes and whether a full assessment is recommended.

2. An assessment A full assessment looks at oral motor skills, texture tolerance, swallowing safety, and the mealtime environment, so we understand exactly what is driving the difficulty.

3. A therapy plan built around your child Every plan is built around the individual child, their pace, and their specific triggers, never a generic one-size-fits-all programme.

4. Sessions that fit your life Feeding therapy works best close to real mealtimes, so sessions are available in the format that suits your family:

Home visits. Sessions at your own table, in the setting where mealtimes actually happen.
School visits. Therapy delivered at school, where relevant, in coordination with your child's day.
Private sessions. In-person sessions at our private premises in Morningside, Sandton.
Online therapy. Remote sessions via video call for guidance, coaching, and follow-up between in-person visits.

5. Family involvement, every step Feeding is a family experience. Parents and caregivers are treated as partners, not bystanders, with practical strategies you can use at every meal, not just in session.
