# Special Needs Speech Therapy

## Everyone deserves to be heard. Every family deserves to be connected.

Special needs speech therapy supports children and adults whose communication needs go beyond typical speech and language development, including complex communication needs, autism spectrum communication differences, and conditions that call for augmentative or alternative approaches. It is a specialist area, and one Jodi has built her career around.

Jodi holds a Master's degree in Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) from the University of Pretoria, and spent four years embedded in a government school for children with autism. That experience shaped her belief that every person is capable of communicating when given the right tools, the right environment, and someone who truly believes in them.

## What special needs speech therapy addresses

**Complex communication needs**
Some people communicate in ways that go beyond speech, and that is okay. We build individualised strategies around how a person communicates best, not around a standard template.

**Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC)**
AAC covers the tools and strategies that support communication beyond speech, including picture-based systems and high-tech communication devices. We help children and adults find the right system and learn to use it with confidence, at home, at school, and out in the world.

**Makaton signing**
A structured signing system used alongside speech to support understanding and expression, especially valuable for children who are not yet reliably verbal.

**The PROMPT system**
A hands-on approach to building the physical movements behind speech, used with children and adults who need extra support developing clear, accurate sound production.

**Autism spectrum communication support**
Communication support shaped around how each person on the spectrum communicates and learns best, built on years of embedded, in-school experience rather than theory alone.

With words, with signs, with anything that works, the goal is always the same: to give someone the ability to communicate their needs.

## How it works

**1. A free screening**
We start with a free screening, with no cost and no commitment required, to understand how someone currently communicates and where support could help most.

**2. An assessment**
A full assessment looks closely at communication across environments, not just in a clinical setting, so the picture we build is a real one.

**3. A therapy plan built around the individual**
Every plan is built around the person's needs, their pace, and the people and places in their world. There is no single AAC system or approach that fits everyone.

**4. Sessions that fit your life**
Therapy should fit your life, not the other way around. Sessions are available in four formats:

- **School visits.** Therapy delivered at school, alongside teachers and support staff who see the child every day.
- **Home visits.** Sessions in your home, where the whole family can learn and participate together.
- **Private sessions.** In-person sessions at our private premises in Morningside, Sandton.
- **Online therapy.** Remote sessions via video call for those who prefer it or where travel is not practical.

**5. A partnership with your family and your team**
Therapy does not stop when the session ends. We work alongside families, schools, and other professionals involved in a person's care, so strategies are used consistently everywhere they are needed.
