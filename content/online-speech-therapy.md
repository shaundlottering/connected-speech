Online Speech Therapy
Therapy that fits your life, not the other way around.

Online speech therapy delivers the same individualised, specialist care as an in-person session, through a video call instead of a shared room. It suits families and adults who prefer to avoid travel, who live outside Sandton and Johannesburg, or who simply want therapy to fit into a busy week without adding a commute to it.

Therapy does not stop when the session ends, whether it happens online or in person, and progress is reported to you clearly either way.

What online therapy can help with

Language and literacy development Building vocabulary, sentence structure, and the foundations of reading and writing works well over video, especially with a parent or caregiver nearby to support in between sessions.

Articulation and intelligibility Sound and speech clarity work can be coached effectively online, with clear modelling, practice, and feedback in real time.

Stuttering and stammering Fluency work, confidence building, and practical strategies translate well to a video call, for children and adults alike.

AAC coaching and follow-up For families and individuals already using an AAC system, Makaton, or PROMPT strategies, online sessions are an efficient way to coach, troubleshoot, and build on progress between in-person visits.

Stroke and medical condition support For adults recovering from a stroke or managing a medical condition, online sessions are often the easiest way to keep therapy consistent, particularly when travel is difficult.

Some needs are better suited to in-person care, particularly early feeding therapy and initial hands-on assessments. If that is the case for you, we will say so honestly and recommend the right format.

How it works

1. A free screening We start with a free screening, available online, with no cost and no commitment required, to understand what is happening and whether a full assessment is recommended.

2. An assessment Assessments can often be completed online too, giving us a clear, accurate picture of what is going on before we build a plan.

3. A therapy plan built around you Every plan is built around the individual's needs and their pace, adapted for the online format so that it is just as targeted as an in-person plan.

4. A simple, private video call Sessions run on a straightforward video call platform. All you need is a device with a camera, a stable internet connection, and a quiet space to sit for the session.

5. Progress you can see Between sessions, you will receive clear guidance on what to practise at home or at school, and progress is reported specifically and honestly, every step of the way.
