Speech Therapy
You have something to say. Let's help you say it.

Speech therapy is the process of building the skills someone needs to communicate clearly and confidently, whether that means finding the right sounds, the right words, the right sentence structure, or the right tool to express a thought. At Connected Speech, therapy is built around the individual: their needs, their pace, and their world.

We work with children from birth through school age, and with adults at any stage of life, including those recovering from a stroke or living with a lifelong communication difference.

What speech therapy addresses

Language delays When language milestones are not being met, early intervention makes the biggest difference. We identify what is holding a child back and build the specific skills they need to communicate their needs, connect with others, and thrive at school.

Language and literacy development Strong language skills are the foundation for reading and writing. We build the vocabulary, sentence structure, and phonological awareness a child needs to develop literacy skills and succeed at school.

Articulation and intelligibility Speech that is difficult to understand can affect confidence and connection. We work on accurate sound production and clear speech patterns, so a person can be understood the first time, by anyone.

Stuttering and stammering Stuttering and stammering affect both children and adults, and the impact goes far beyond speech. Your therapist works with you and your family to build fluency, confidence, and the tools to communicate without fear, at any age.

Stroke and medical conditions We support adults recovering from strokes and other medical conditions that affect communication, helping them rebuild the skills they need to express themselves and reconnect with the people in their lives.

Complex communication needs Some people communicate in ways that go beyond speech, and that is okay. We support children and adults with AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication), Makaton signing, the PROMPT system, and individualised strategies, so they can express themselves and participate fully in the world around them. With words, with signs, with anything that works.

How it works

1. A free screening Every journey starts with a free screening, available for individuals and for schools, with no cost and no commitment required. A screening gives a clear picture of whether a full assessment is recommended.

2. An assessment If a full assessment is recommended, we take the time to understand exactly what is happening, what is driving it, and what a realistic path forward looks like.

3. A therapy plan built around you No two therapy plans look the same. Sessions are built around the individual's needs, their pace, and the environments where they actually live and communicate.

4. Sessions that fit your life Therapy should fit your life, not the other way around. Sessions are available in four formats:

School visits. Therapy delivered at the child's school, in the environment where learning happens.
Home visits. Sessions in your home, where you are relaxed and the whole family can participate.
Private sessions. In-person sessions at our private premises in Morningside, Sandton.
Online therapy. Remote sessions via video call for those who prefer it or where travel is not practical.

5. Progress you can see Therapy does not stop when the session ends. Families and schools are kept informed every step of the way, with clear, specific reporting on progress, not vague updates.
