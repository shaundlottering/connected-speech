FOR SCHOOLS
Onsite Speech-Language Therapy
	

Bring speech therapy onto your campus,
without the logistics falling on you.
Connected Speech is a private speech-language pathology practice offering onsite therapy for schools in Sandton and across Johannesburg. Sessions happen in your classrooms and therapy spaces, on a day that suits your timetable, run by a single dedicated therapist your staff and families get to know by name.
WHY SCHOOLS PARTNER WITH CONNECTED SPEECH
On your campus
Therapy in the classroom or a quiet space you provide, so there is no travel and no missed lesson time chasing appointments.
	Free screenings
Group or individual screenings for learners of concern, with no cost and no commitment attached.
	Specialist in AAC
Deep expertise in complex communication needs, Makaton, PROMPT and augmentative devices, not just articulation.
	Medical aid handled
Claims are submitted on the family's behalf, so parents are not left to chase paperwork.
	

WHAT WE SUPPORT AT SCHOOL
* Language delays and language & literacy development
* Articulation and speech intelligibility
* Complex communication needs & AAC (Makaton, PROMPT, devices)
* Feeding and oral motor difficulties
* Stuttering and stammering, at any age
	

	HOW A PARTNERSHIP WORKS
1.  A short call to understand your learners’ needs and your timetable.
2.  Free screening for identified learners, either individual or group.
3.  A simple onsite schedule is set, weekly, Monday to Thursday.
4.  Sessions run at school. Medical aid claims are submitted directly to parents’ funds.
5.  Progress is reported clearly to you and to families, every step.
